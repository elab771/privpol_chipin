Privacy Policy for ChipIn

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how ChipIn ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Summary

ChipIn is designed to work entirely offline. We do not operate a server, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal or financial data to us or to any third party for our own use.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, financial account information, or the details of your expenses and groups. All group, expense, and settlement data you enter into ChipIn is stored locally on your device only, using the device's local database. We have no access to it.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Camera: Used only if you choose to attach a photo (e.g., a receipt) to an expense. Photos are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded anywhere by us.
  • Notifications: Used to send you local reminders about outstanding balances within your own groups. These notifications are generated on your device and are not based on any data sent to a server.

In-App Purchases

ChipIn offers an optional one-time "Support ChipIn" purchase to unlock Pro features. This purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — that information is handled directly by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation of whether the purchase was completed, which we use to unlock Pro features on your device.

Data Storage and Backup

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you have Android's automatic backup enabled, your device's operating system (not ChipIn) may include this local data in encrypted backups to your own Google account, as it would for any app, per Android's backup policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing — for processing the optional in-app purchase (see above).
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs at this time.

Children's Privacy

ChipIn is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children, as we do not collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
